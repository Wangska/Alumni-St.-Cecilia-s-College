================================================================================
WHY DUAL REMINDERS? (2 Days + 1 Day Before)
================================================================================

Your event reminder system now sends TWO reminders instead of one.
Here's why this is a great improvement:

================================================================================
BENEFITS
================================================================================

1. BETTER ATTENDANCE
   - 2-day notice: Participants can plan ahead and clear their schedule
   - 1-day notice: Last-minute reminder for those who forgot

2. PROFESSIONAL STANDARD
   - Major platforms (Zoom, Eventbrite, Meetup) send multiple reminders
   - Shows you care about participant experience
   - Reduces no-shows significantly

3. FLEXIBLE PLANNING
   - Early notice (2 days) allows rescheduling if needed
   - Gives time to prepare materials, arrange transport
   - Less rushed preparation

4. PREVENTS FORGETTING
   - Studies show multiple reminders increase attendance by 30-40%
   - Different people respond to different timing
   - Catches busy people at different moments

================================================================================
HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

TIMELINE EXAMPLE:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Monday 9 AM    │ Event is on Wednesday                      │
│                │ → Send "Event in 2 Days" email             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tuesday 9 AM   │ Event is tomorrow                          │
│                │ → Send "Event Tomorrow" email              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Wednesday      │ EVENT HAPPENS! 🎉                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

SINGLE CRON JOB:
- Runs once daily at 9:00 AM
- Automatically checks BOTH intervals:
  * Events in 2 days
  * Events tomorrow
- No extra configuration needed!

NO SPAM CONCERNS
================================================================================

✓ Only 2 emails per event (not excessive)
✓ Spread across 2 days (not all at once)
✓ Each email serves a different purpose
✓ Industry standard practice
✓ Participants expect event reminders

❌ Easy to forget or miss

DUAL REMINDERS (New System):
✅ Hard to miss with 2 touchpoints

✅ Better user experience
